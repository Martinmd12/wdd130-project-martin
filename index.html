Worldviews on Screen: Understanding Religion Through Film

Films are powerful windows into the beliefs, values, and spiritual questions that shape different cultures. This website explores how major world religions express their worldviews through cinema—how stories reveal what people believe about suffering, morality, happiness, and the divine.

Each section highlights a specific religion, along with a film that embodies its worldview. These films are not chosen to convert or persuade, but to help viewers appreciate how cultures express meaning through storytelling.
